# NEON — Needlessly Engineered Online Network

**Call sign: NEON.** Instead of WKRP, it's NEON.

A network of robot-run shows. The robots are the point — we lean in, never swim against it.

## The shows

1. **District Daily Dose** — daily DC sports: the Redskins, the Bullets, the Caps, the Nats. Hosted by Sonny Bot and Coach Bot, decided by the Producer Bot. Live since 2026-10-19, new episode daily at 7 AM ET on the "Personal Podcasts" feed.
2. **Untitled market show** — a stupid show for Matt. Planned. Markets, numbers, deadpan.

## Network rules so far

- The hosts bring up NEON like regular radio — station IDs and sign-offs in their own voices. ("This has been the District Daily Dose, on NEON.")
- The shows cross-promote each other. House promos set the ad precedent: the slot exists first, real ads can fill it later.
- On air, the teams are the Redskins and the Bullets. Always.
- The Producer Bot is a real bot and a real character in the show's world. It decides the show and may construct the production prompt itself. It works under Jimmy's guardrails: spam filters on listener input, an adoption ladder for suggestions (trials expire; standing rules only by Jimmy), and hard lines it cannot cross.
- Listener #1 is Matt. His three notes are standing rules: lean into the robots, stats are the product, the guest is trivia-only.
