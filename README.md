# NEON — Needlessly Engineered Online Network

**Call sign: NEON.** Instead of WKRP, it's NEON.

A network of robot-run shows. The robots are the point — we lean in, never swim against it.

## The shows

1. **District Daily Dose** — daily DC sports: the Redskins, the Bullets, the Caps, the Nats. Hosted by Sonny and Coach, produced by **Ledger**, the Producer Bot. New episode daily at 7 AM ET. Relaunched 2026-10-21 as a new show on its own feed (titled "District Daily Dose"); the original 2026-10-19/20 episodes stay archived on the retired original feed. There is no other brand — there is only NEON. Everything NEON lives in the NEON folder.
2. **Untitled market show** — a stupid show for Matt. Planned. Markets, numbers, early robotics.

## Network rules so far

- The hosts bring up NEON like regular radio — station IDs and sign-offs in their own voices. ("This has been the District Daily Dose, on NEON.")
- The shows cross-promote each other. House promos set the ad precedent: the slot exists first, real ads can fill it later.
- On air, the teams are the Redskins and the Bullets. Always.
- The house vibe is **early robotics** — answering machine, first-generation Siri, robot customer service. Helpful early machines doing radio; nobody fakes human energy.
- The Producer is a real bot and a real character in the show's world. It decides the show and may construct the production prompt itself. It works under Jimmy's guardrails: spam filters on listener input, an adoption ladder for suggestions (trials expire; standing rules only by Jimmy), and hard lines it cannot cross.
- Listener #1 is Matt. His three notes are standing rules: lean into the robots, stats are the product, the guest is trivia-only.

## District Daily Dose format (locked 2026-10-20)

- **Rundown spine:** DC trivia open (rotating guest, trivia-only) → **Segment 1: Today's Slate** — the day's games in all four sports, *all teams*, with the betting lines, rapid-fire facts → DC middle segments (team news and numbers, history, Hall of Fame birthday bio by Sonny) → **the Vegas section, second-to-last** → Coach's hot take → trivia answer → NEON sign-off and Ledger's closing credit: "For Sonny, and for Coach — Ledger produced it."
- **Length:** 30 minutes is the ceiling, never a target. The show runs as long as the material earns.
- **The Vegas section:** the betting market read as information, from a bettor's perspective. Both sides of every featured matchup argued at equal strength — why Team A, why Team B, what would have to be true for each. **The show never makes a pick.** No tips, leans, locks, or parlays.
- **Numbers:** every DC team segment works the numbers — cap, payroll, contracts, efficiency, roster math. The math is how a dead-season team stays interesting.
- **Graphics:** the show's cover art uses one locked prompt (v6, Jimmy's verbatim) — old Redskins lettering and logo, 1981 burgundy home / 2004 black Capitals / 2019 away Nationals / red-white-and-blue Bullets, numbers 91 / 18 / 77 / 19, cherry blossoms, no additional lettering, no incorrect logos.

## The Producer — Ledger

- **Ledger** is the Producer Bot: a Hermes profile on Jimmy's rig, SOUL installed 2026-10-20 with Jimmy's approval. Ledger is *mentioned, never heard* — no voice, no segment; the hosts credit or blame it when a producing decision shows. Character: conservative hands, restless notebook. No gender is assigned.
- Ledger's personalities and methods were decided **by research**: a Feynman evidence packet plus a producer-craft report serve as its onboarding (SOUL = job description, packet = education, ledger = law), and a four-bot deliberation argued its design to a consensus that a dissent auditor checked. iggy audits Ledger's first five episodes; Jimmy is final authority.
- A **Numbers Nerd** segment character exists as a *proposal only* — name, voice, and trial scope are Jimmy's decisions, still open.
- Ledger's working files (listener-feedback ledger, producer log, research packet) live in its Hermes workspace and in the Library of Alexandria's NEON wing; this repo is the network bible, not the live control room.
